Welcome to Iceland

A two-day route through the land of fire and ice — from bubbling geysers and thundering waterfalls to black sand shores.

Day One

The Golden Circle

Your first day will take you through Iceland's most iconic landmarks, featuring geysers, waterfalls, and dramatic geological formations.

  1. Þingvellir National Park
    • UNESCO World Heritage Site
    • Walk between continental plates
    • Stunning valley views
  2. Geysir & Strokkur
    • Watch Strokkur geyser erupt
    • Visit geothermal hot springs
  3. Gullfoss Waterfall
    • Marvel at the powerful cascade
    • Known as the "Golden Waterfall"
  4. Kerið Crater
    • Visit a colorful volcanic crater
    • Blue crater lake
Day Two

South Coast Adventure

Experience the dramatic southern coast with massive waterfalls, black sand beaches, and striking rock formations.

  1. Seljalandsfoss Waterfall
    • Walk behind the waterfall
    • Unique perspective of cascading water
  2. Skógafoss Waterfall
    • Feel the mist of massive falls
    • One of Iceland's most powerful waterfalls
  3. Reynisfjara Black Sand Beach
    • See dramatic black sand
    • Towering basalt columns
  4. Vík & Skaftafell
    • Visit Iceland's southernmost town
    • Experience local culture

Quick Facts About Iceland

  • Capital: Reykjavík
  • Language: Icelandic
  • Currency: Icelandic Króna (ISK)
  • Population: Around 390,000 people
  • Time zone: GMT (UTC+0)
  • Best time to visit: May to September
  • Northern Lights season: September to April
